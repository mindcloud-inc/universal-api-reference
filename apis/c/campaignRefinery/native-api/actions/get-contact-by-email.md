# Get Contact by Email with Campaign Refinery

Retrieves a contact by email from Campaign Refinery.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/contacts/get-contact`
- **Base URL:** `https://app.campaignrefinery.com/rest`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Contact by Email](https://developers.campaignrefinery.com/reference/get-contact)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | query | `string` | yes | Contact email address to look up. |
