# List People And Company Contact Stages with Cloze

Retrieves contact stages from Cloze.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v1/user/stages/people`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.cloze.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List People And Company Contact Stages](https://api.cloze.com/api-docs/#/paths/v1-user-stages-people/get)
