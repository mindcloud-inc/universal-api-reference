# Assign Designs To Organization with Corporate Merch

Assigns designs to an organization in Corporate Merch.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/v2/organizations/{organization}/designs`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.corporatemerch.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Assign Designs To Organization](https://corporatemerch.readme.io/reference/assign-design-to-organization)
