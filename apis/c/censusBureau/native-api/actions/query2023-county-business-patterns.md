# Query 2023 County Business Patterns with Census Bureau

Queries Census Bureau 2023 county business patterns.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/2023/cbp`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.census.gov/data`
- **Official documentation:** [Query 2023 County Business Patterns](https://api.census.gov/data/2023/cbp.html)
