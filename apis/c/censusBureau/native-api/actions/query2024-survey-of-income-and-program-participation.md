# Query 2024 Survey of Income and Program Participation with Census Bureau

Queries Census Bureau 2024 Survey of Income and Program Participation.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/2024/sipp`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.census.gov/data`
- **Official documentation:** [Query 2024 Survey of Income and Program Participation](https://api.census.gov/data/2024/sipp.html)
