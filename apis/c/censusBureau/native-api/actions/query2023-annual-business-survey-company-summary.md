# Query 2023 Annual Business Survey Company Summary with Census Bureau

Queries Census Bureau 2023 annual business survey company summary data.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/2023/abscs`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.census.gov/data`
- **Official documentation:** [Query 2023 Annual Business Survey Company Summary](https://api.census.gov/data/2023/abscs.html)
