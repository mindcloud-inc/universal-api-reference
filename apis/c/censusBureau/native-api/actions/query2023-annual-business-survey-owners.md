# Query 2023 Annual Business Survey Owners with Census Bureau

Queries Census Bureau 2023 annual business survey owners data.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/2023/abscbo`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.census.gov/data`
- **Official documentation:** [Query 2023 Annual Business Survey Owners](https://api.census.gov/data/2023/abscbo.html)
