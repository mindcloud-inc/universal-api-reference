# Query 2023 Annual Integrated Economic Survey Nonemployer with Census Bureau

Queries Census Bureau 2023 annual integrated economic survey nonemployer data.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/2023/aiesnonemp`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.census.gov/data`
- **Official documentation:** [Query 2023 Annual Integrated Economic Survey Nonemployer](https://api.census.gov/data/2023/aiesnonemp.html)
