# List Contract Administration News Feed Items with City of Tampa, Florida

Retrieves contract administration news feed items from City of Tampa, Florida.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/news/feed/rfqs`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.tampa.gov`
- **Official documentation:** [List Contract Administration News Feed Items](https://www.tampa.gov/info/rss-feeds)
