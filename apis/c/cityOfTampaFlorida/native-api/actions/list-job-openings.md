# List Job Openings with City of Tampa, Florida

Retrieves job openings from City of Tampa, Florida.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://jobapscloud.com/Tampa/rss.asp`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.tampa.gov`
- **Official documentation:** [List Job Openings](https://www.tampa.gov/info/rss-feeds)
