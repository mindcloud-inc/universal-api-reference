# Retrieve Business Entity with ChargeBee

Retrieves a business entity from ChargeBee.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `business_entities/:business_entity_id`
- **Base URL:** `https://{baseUrl}.chargebee.com/api/v2/`
- **Official documentation:** [Retrieve Business Entity](https://apidocs.chargebee.com/docs/api/business-entities/retrieve-a-business-entity)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `business_entity_id` | path | `string` | yes | The Chargebee business entity identifier. |
