# Kling Video To Audio with CometAPI

Creates audio from video with Kling in CometAPI.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/kling/v1/audio/video-to-audio`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.cometapi.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Kling Video To Audio](https://apidoc.cometapi.com/api/video/kling/video-to-audio)
