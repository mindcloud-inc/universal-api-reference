# Get Business Account Details with Fliqr AI

Retrieves business account details from Fliqr AI.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/accounts/me`
- **Base URL:** `https://app.fliqr.ai/api/`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Business Account Details](https://docs.fliqr.ai/api-reference/accounts/get-accountsme)
