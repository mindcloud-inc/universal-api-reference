# Get Business Statistics with Explorium

Retrieves business statistics from Explorium API.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/v1/businesses/stats`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.explorium.ai`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Business Statistics](https://developers.explorium.ai/reference/businesses/fetch_businesses_statistics)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `filters` | body | `object` | yes | The filters object that scopes the business statistics request. |
