# Get Recently Used Product Swatches with Ecwid

Retrieves recently used product swatches from Ecwid.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/:storeId/swatches`
- **Base URL:** `https://app.ecwid.com/api/v3`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Recently Used Product Swatches](https://docs.ecwid.com/api-reference/rest-api/products/get-recently-used-product-swatches)
