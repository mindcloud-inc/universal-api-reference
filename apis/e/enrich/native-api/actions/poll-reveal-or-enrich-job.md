# Poll Reveal Or Enrich Job with Enrich.so

Retrieves reveal or enrich job status from Enrich.so.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/lead-finder/reveal-jobs/{jobId}`
- **Base URL:** `https://dev.enrich.so/api/v3`
- **Official documentation:** [Poll Reveal Or Enrich Job](https://doc.enrich.so/poll-revealenrich-job-29163791e0.md)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `jobId` | path | `string` | yes | Reveal or enrich job ID. |
