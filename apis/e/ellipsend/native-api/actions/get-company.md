# Get Company with Ellipsend

Retrieves your company details from Ellipsend.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://api.ellipsend.com/v1/company`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.ellipsend.com/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company](https://api.ellipsend.com/v1/docs#/Company/get_v1_company)
