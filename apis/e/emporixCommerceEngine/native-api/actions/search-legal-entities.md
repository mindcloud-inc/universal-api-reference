# Search Legal Entities with Emporix Commerce Engine

Finds legal entities in Emporix Commerce Engine by search criteria.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/customer-management/{tenantId}/legal-entities/search`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.emporix.io`
- **Official documentation:** [Search Legal Entities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emporix/api-references/refs/heads/main/companies-and-customers/client-management/api-reference/api.yml)
