# Get Legal Entity with Emporix Commerce Engine

Retrieves a legal entity from Emporix Commerce Engine.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/customer-management/{tenantId}/legal-entities/:legalEntityId`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.emporix.io`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Legal Entity](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emporix/api-references/refs/heads/main/companies-and-customers/client-management/api-reference/api.yml)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `legalEntityId` | path | `string` | yes | The unique ID of the legal entity. |
