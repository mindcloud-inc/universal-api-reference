# Get Email Verification Job with EmailListVerify

Retrieves email verification job status and results from EmailListVerify.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/api/emailJobs/:id`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.emaillistverify.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Email Verification Job](https://api.emaillistverify.com/api-doc#/Verification%20Endpoints/getEmailJob)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | path | `string` | yes | Email verification job ID. |
