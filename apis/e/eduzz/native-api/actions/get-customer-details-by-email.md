# Get Customer Details by Email with Eduzz

Retrieves customer details from Eduzz by email address.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/myeduzz/v1/subscriptions/customers/:email`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.eduzz.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Customer Details by Email](https://developers.eduzz.com/reference/api/get-myeduzz-v1-subscriptions-customers-email)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | path | `string` | yes | Customer email address. |
