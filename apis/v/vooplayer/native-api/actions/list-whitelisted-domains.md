# List Whitelisted Domains with Vooplayer

Retrieves whitelisted domains from your Vooplayer account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/api/user/domain`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.spotlightr.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Whitelisted Domains](https://app.spotlightr.com/docs/api/#getDomain)
