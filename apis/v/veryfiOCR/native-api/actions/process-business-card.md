# Process Business Card with Veryfi OCR

- **Path:** `/api/v8/partner/business-cards`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.veryfi.com/`
- **Official documentation:** [Process Business Card](https://docs.veryfi.com/api/business-cards/process-a-business-card/)
