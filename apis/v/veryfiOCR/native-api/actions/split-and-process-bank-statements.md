# Split And Process Bank Statements with Veryfi OCR

Processes split bank statements in Veryfi OCR.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/api/v8/partner/bank-statements-set`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.veryfi.com/`
- **Official documentation:** [Split And Process Bank Statements](https://docs.veryfi.com/api/split-and-process-multiple-bank-statements/)
