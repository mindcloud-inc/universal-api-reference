# Get Business Cards with Veryfi OCR

Retrieves business cards from Veryfi OCR.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/api/v8/partner/business-cards`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.veryfi.com/`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Business Cards](https://docs.veryfi.com/api/business-cards/get-business-cards/)
