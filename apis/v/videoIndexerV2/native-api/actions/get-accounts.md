# Get Accounts with Video Indexer (V2)

Retrieves accounts from Video Indexer (V2).

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/Auth/trial/Accounts`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.videoindexer.ai`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Accounts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/connectors/videoindexer-v2/#get-accounts)
