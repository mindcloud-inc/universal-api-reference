# Send Customer Invoice Email with Visma eAccounting

Sends a customer invoice email from Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/customerinvoices/{invoiceId}/email`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Send Customer Invoice Email](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#customerinvoices/post/customerinvoices/{invoiceId}/email)
