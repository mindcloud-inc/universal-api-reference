# Get Article with Visma eAccounting

Retrieves an article from Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/articles/{articleId}`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Article](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#articles/get/articles/{articleId})
