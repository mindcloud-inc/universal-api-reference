# Get Customer Invoice Draft with Visma eAccounting

Retrieves a customer invoice draft from Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/customerinvoicedrafts/{invoiceDraftId}`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Customer Invoice Draft](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#customerinvoicedrafts/get/customerinvoicedrafts/{invoiceDraftId})
