# Send Customer Invoice Payment Reminder with Visma eAccounting

Creates a payment reminder for a customer invoice in Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/customerinvoices/{invoiceId}/paymentreminders`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Send Customer Invoice Payment Reminder](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#customerinvoices/post/customerinvoices/{invoiceId}/paymentreminders)
