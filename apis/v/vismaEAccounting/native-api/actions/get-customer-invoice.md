# Get Customer Invoice with Visma eAccounting

Retrieves a customer invoice from Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/customerinvoices/{invoiceId}`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Customer Invoice](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#customerinvoices/get/customerinvoices/{invoiceId})
