# Convert Quote To Customer Invoice with Visma eAccounting

Creates a customer invoice from a quote in Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/quotes/{id}/converttocustomerinvoice`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Convert Quote To Customer Invoice](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#quotes/post/quotes/{id}/converttocustomerinvoice)
