# Convert Customer Invoice Draft To Customer Invoice with Visma eAccounting

Creates a customer invoice from a draft in Visma eAccounting.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/customerinvoicedrafts/{customerInvoiceDraftId}/convert`
- **Base URL:** `https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Convert Customer Invoice Draft To Customer Invoice](https://eaccountingapi.vismaonline.com/scalar/v2#customerinvoicedrafts/post/customerinvoicedrafts/{customerInvoiceDraftId}/convert)
