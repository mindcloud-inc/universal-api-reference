# Find CRM Contact by Phone with WhatsScale

Finds a CRM contact in WhatsScale by phone number.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/api/crm/contacts/phone/:phone`
- **Base URL:** `https://proxy.whatsscale.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Find CRM Contact by Phone](https://whatsscale.com/docs)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `phone` | path | `string` | yes | Phone number to find. |
