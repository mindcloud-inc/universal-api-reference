# Delete Business with GatherUp

Deletes an existing business from GatherUp.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/business/delete`
- **Base URL:** `https://app.gatherup.com/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Delete Business](https://app.gatherup.com/api/doc/business/delete)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `businessId` | body | `number` | yes | Business id. |
