# Get Customer Survey Answers with GatherUp

Retrieves customer survey answers from GatherUp.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/survey-questions/customer/get`
- **Base URL:** `https://app.gatherup.com/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Customer Survey Answers](https://app.gatherup.com/api/doc/survey-questions/customer/get)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `customerId` | body | `number` | yes | Customer id. |
