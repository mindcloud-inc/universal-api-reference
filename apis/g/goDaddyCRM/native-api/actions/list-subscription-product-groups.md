# List Subscription Product Groups with GoDaddy CRM

Retrieves subscription product groups from GoDaddy.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v1/subscriptions/productGroups`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.godaddy.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Subscription Product Groups](https://developer.godaddy.com/doc/endpoint/subscriptions)
