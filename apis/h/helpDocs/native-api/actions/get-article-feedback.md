# Get Article Feedback with HelpDocs

Retrieves article feedback from HelpDocs.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/stats/feedback`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.helpdocs.io/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Article Feedback](https://apidocs.helpdocs.io/article/xvypxgyg1x-article-feedback)
