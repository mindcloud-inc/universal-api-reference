# Create Or Transfer Conversation with MindMe

Creates or transfers a conversation in MindMe.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/api/Conversation/CreateOrTransferConversation`
- **Base URL:** `https://prodapi.mindmemobile.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Create Or Transfer Conversation](https://prodapi.mindmemobile.com/swagger/v1/swagger.json#/paths/~1api~1Conversation~1CreateOrTransferConversation/post)
