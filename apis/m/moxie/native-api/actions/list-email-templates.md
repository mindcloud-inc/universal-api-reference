# List Email Templates with Moxie

Retrieves email templates from Moxie.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/action/emailTemplates/list`
- **Base URL:** `https://pod01.withmoxie.com/api/public`
- **Official documentation:** [List Email Templates](https://help.withmoxie.com/en/articles/8260211-list-email-templates)
