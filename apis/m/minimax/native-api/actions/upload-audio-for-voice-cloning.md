# Upload Audio for Voice Cloning with Minimax

Uploads voice-cloning audio to Minimax.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/v1/files/upload`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.minimax.io`
- **Official documentation:** [Upload Audio for Voice Cloning](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/voice-cloning-uploadcloneaudio)
