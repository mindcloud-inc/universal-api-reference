# Get Terms And Conditions with Moodo & Moodo AIR

Retrieves the Terms and Conditions text from Moodo.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/terms_and_conditions`
- **Base URL:** `https://rest.moodo.co/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Terms And Conditions](https://rest.moodo.co/#/texts/get_terms_and_conditions)
