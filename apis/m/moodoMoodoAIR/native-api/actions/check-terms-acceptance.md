# Check Terms Acceptance with Moodo & Moodo AIR

Checks whether Terms and Conditions were previously accepted.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `PATCH`
- **Path:** `/terms_and_conditions`
- **Base URL:** `https://rest.moodo.co/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Check Terms Acceptance](https://rest.moodo.co/#/texts/check_terms_and_conditions)
