# Create a company with Middesk

Creates a company in your Middesk account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/partner/companies`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.middesk.com/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Create a company](https://docs.middesk.com/docs/jurisdiction-registration-flow)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `legal_name` | body | `string` | yes | Legal name of the company to create. |
