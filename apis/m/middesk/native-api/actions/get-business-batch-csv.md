# Download business batch CSV with Middesk

Retrieves a business batch CSV from Middesk.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/business_batches/:id/csv`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.middesk.com/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Download business batch CSV](https://docs.middesk.com/reference/businesses)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | path | `string` | yes | Business batch ID |
