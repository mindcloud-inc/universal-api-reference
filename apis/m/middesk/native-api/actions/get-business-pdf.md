# Retrieve a business PDF with Middesk

Retrieves a business PDF from Middesk.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/businesses/:id/pdf`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.middesk.com/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Retrieve a business PDF](https://docs.middesk.com/reference/businesses)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | path | `string` | yes | ID of the business whose PDF you want to retrieve. |
