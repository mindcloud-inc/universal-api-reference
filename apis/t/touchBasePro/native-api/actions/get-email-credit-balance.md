# Get Email Credit Balance with TouchBasePro

Retrieves email credit balance from TouchBasePro.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/email/balance`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.touchbasepro.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Email Credit Balance](https://developer.touchbasepro.com/api-details#api=tbp-email-api)
