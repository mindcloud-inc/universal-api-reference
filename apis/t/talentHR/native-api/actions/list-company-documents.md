# List Company Documents with TalentHR

Retrieves company documents from TalentHR.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/documents/company-documents`
- **Base URL:** `https://pubapi.talenthr.io/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [List Company Documents](https://apidocs.talenthr.io/#d0c0cca0-e32d-4762-88ca-25e92e1121e3)
