# Get Email Reports with Temp Stick

Retrieves Temp Stick email report settings and downloadable reports.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/user/email-reports`
- **Base URL:** `https://tempstickapi.com/api/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Email Reports](https://tempstickapi.com/docs/#api-User-Get_Email_Reports)
