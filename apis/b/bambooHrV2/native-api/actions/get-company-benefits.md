# Get Company Benefits with BambooHR

Retrieves company benefit plans from BambooHR.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v1/benefit/company_benefit`
- **Base URL:** `https://mindcloud.bamboohr.com/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company Benefits](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/reference/get-company-benefits-1)
