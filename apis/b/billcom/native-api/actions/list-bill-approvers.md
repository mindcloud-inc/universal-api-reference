# List Approvers Assigned to a Bill with BILL Payables & Receivables

Retrieves approvers assigned to a bill in Bill.com.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `ListApprovers.json`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.bill.com/api/v2`
- **Official documentation:** [List Approvers Assigned to a Bill](https://developer.bill.com/v2/reference/ap-approvals-listapprovers)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `objectId` | body | `string` | yes |
