# Get Contact with Bland AI

Retrieves a contact from your Bland AI account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v1/contacts/{contact_id}`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.bland.ai`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Contact](https://docs.bland.ai/api-v1/get/contacts-id)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `contact_id` | path | `string` | yes |
