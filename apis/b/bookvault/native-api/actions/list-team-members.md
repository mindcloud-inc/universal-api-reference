# List Team Members with Bookvault

Retrieves team members from your Bookvault account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/Team`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.bookvault.app/v3`
- **Official documentation:** [List Team Members](https://api.bookvault.app/v3/docs#tag/Account/operation/Team_get)
