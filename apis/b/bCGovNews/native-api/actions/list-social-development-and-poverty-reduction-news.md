# List Social Development and Poverty Reduction News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Social Development and Poverty Reduction announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/social-development-and-poverty-reduction/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Social Development and Poverty Reduction News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/social-development-and-poverty-reduction/feed)
