# List Jobs and Economic Growth News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Jobs and Economic Growth announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/jobs-and-economic-growth/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Jobs and Economic Growth News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/jobs-and-economic-growth/feed)
