# List Emergency Management and Climate Readiness News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Emergency Management and Climate Readiness announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/emergency-management-and-climate-readiness/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Emergency Management and Climate Readiness News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/emergency-management-and-climate-readiness/feed)
