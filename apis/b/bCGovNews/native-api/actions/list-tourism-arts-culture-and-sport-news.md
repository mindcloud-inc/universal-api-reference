# List Tourism Arts Culture and Sport News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Tourism Arts Culture and Sport announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/tourism-arts-culture-and-sport/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Tourism Arts Culture and Sport News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/tourism-arts-culture-and-sport/feed)
