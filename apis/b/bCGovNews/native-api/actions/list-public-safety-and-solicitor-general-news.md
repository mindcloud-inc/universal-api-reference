# List Public Safety and Solicitor General News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Public Safety and Solicitor General announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/public-safety-and-solicitor-general/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Public Safety and Solicitor General News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/public-safety-and-solicitor-general/feed)
