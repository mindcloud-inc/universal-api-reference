# List Office of the Premier News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Office of the Premier announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/office-of-the-premier/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Office of the Premier News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/office-of-the-premier/feed)
