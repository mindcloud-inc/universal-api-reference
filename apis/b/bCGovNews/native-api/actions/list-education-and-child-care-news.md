# List Education and Child Care News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Education and Child Care announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/education-and-child-care/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Education and Child Care News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/education-and-child-care/feed)
