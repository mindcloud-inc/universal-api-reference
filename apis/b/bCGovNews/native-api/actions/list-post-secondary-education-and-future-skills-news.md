# List Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/post-secondary-education-and-future-skills/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/post-secondary-education-and-future-skills/feed)
