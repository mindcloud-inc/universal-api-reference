# List Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/indigenous-relations-and-reconciliation/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/indigenous-relations-and-reconciliation/feed)
