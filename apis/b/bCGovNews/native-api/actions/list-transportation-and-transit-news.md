# List Transportation and Transit News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Transportation and Transit announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/transportation-and-transit/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Transportation and Transit News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/transportation-and-transit/feed)
