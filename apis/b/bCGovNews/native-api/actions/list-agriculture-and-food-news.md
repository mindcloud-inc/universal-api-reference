# List Agriculture and Food News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Agriculture and Food announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/agriculture-and-food/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Agriculture and Food News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/agriculture-and-food/feed)
