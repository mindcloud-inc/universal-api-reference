# List Mining and Critical Minerals News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Mining and Critical Minerals announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/mining-and-critical-minerals/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Mining and Critical Minerals News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/mining-and-critical-minerals/feed)
