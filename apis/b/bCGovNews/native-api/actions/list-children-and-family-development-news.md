# List Children and Family Development News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Children and Family Development announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/children-and-family-development/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Children and Family Development News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/children-and-family-development/feed)
