# List Energy and Climate Solutions News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Energy and Climate Solutions announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/energy-and-climate-solutions/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Energy and Climate Solutions News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/energy-and-climate-solutions/feed)
