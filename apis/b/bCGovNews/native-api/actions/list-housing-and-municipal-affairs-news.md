# List Housing and Municipal Affairs News with BC Gov News

Retrieves Housing and Municipal Affairs announcements from BC Gov News.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/ministries/housing-and-municipal-affairs/feed`
- **Base URL:** `https://news.gov.bc.ca`
- **Official documentation:** [List Housing and Municipal Affairs News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/ministries/housing-and-municipal-affairs/feed)
