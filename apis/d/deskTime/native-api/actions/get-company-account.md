# Get Company Account with DeskTime

Retrieves your company account details from DeskTime.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/company`
- **Base URL:** `https://desktime.com/api/v2/json`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company Account](https://help.desktime.com/hc/en-us/articles/25494763327901-How-to-use-Account-endpoint)
