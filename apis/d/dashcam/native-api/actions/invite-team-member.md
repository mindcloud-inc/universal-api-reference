# Invite Team Member with Dashcam

Invites a team member in Dashcam.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/api/v1/teams/invite-member`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.testdriver.ai`
- **Official documentation:** [Invite Team Member](https://docs.testdriver.ai/v6/account/team)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | body | `string` | no |
| `onlySendEmail` | body | `string` | no |
