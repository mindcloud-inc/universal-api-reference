# Get Research Job with DocsBot AI

Retrieves a research job from DocsBot AI.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/teams/:teamId/bots/:botId/research/:jobId`
- **Base URL:** `https://docsbot.ai/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Research Job](https://docsbot.ai/documentation/developer/bot-research-api)
