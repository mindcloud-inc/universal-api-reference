# Get Company Brand Colors with Ritekit

Retrieves company brand colors from Ritekit.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v2/company-insights/brand-colors`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.ritekit.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company Brand Colors](https://documenter.getpostman.com/view/2010712/SzS7Qku5?version=latest)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | query | `string` | yes | Company name or domain to inspect for brand colors. |
