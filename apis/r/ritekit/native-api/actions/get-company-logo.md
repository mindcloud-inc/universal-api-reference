# Get Company Logo with Ritekit

Retrieves a company logo from Ritekit.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v2/company-insights/logo`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.ritekit.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company Logo](https://documenter.getpostman.com/view/2010712/SzS7Qku5?version=latest)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `domain` | query | `string` | yes | Company domain to fetch a logo for. |
