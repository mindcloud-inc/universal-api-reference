# Assign Customer Offering with RevenueCat

Assigns or clears a customer offering override in RevenueCat.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `projects/:projectId/customers/:customerId/actions/assign_offering`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.revenuecat.com/v2`
- **Official documentation:** [Assign Customer Offering](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2)
