# Transfer Customer with RevenueCat

Transfers a customer's subscriptions and purchases to another RevenueCat customer.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `projects/:projectId/customers/:customerId/actions/transfer`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.revenuecat.com/v2`
- **Official documentation:** [Transfer Customer](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2)
