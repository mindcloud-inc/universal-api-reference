# Upload Legacy Video with Reka AI

Uploads a legacy video to Reka AI.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `https://vision-agent.api.reka.ai/v1/videos/upload`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.reka.ai`
- **Official documentation:** [Upload Legacy Video](https://docs.reka.ai/vision/api-reference/video-management/upload)
