# Upload Video with Reka AI

Uploads a video to Reka AI.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `https://vision-agent.api.reka.ai/v2/videos`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.reka.ai`
- **Official documentation:** [Upload Video](https://docs.reka.ai/vision/api-reference/v-2/upload-video-v-2-videos-post)
