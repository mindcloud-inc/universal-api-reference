# Retrieve total amount of sent emails with Routee

Retrieves total amount of sent emails from Routee.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/smtp/emails/total`
- **Base URL:** `https://connect.routee.net`
- **Official documentation:** [Retrieve total amount of sent emails](https://docs.routee.net/reference/retrieving-total-amount-of-sent-emails)
