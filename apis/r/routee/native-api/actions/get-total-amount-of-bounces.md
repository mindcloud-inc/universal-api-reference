# Get total amount of bounces with Routee

Retrieves total amount of bounces from Routee.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/smtp/bounces/day/total`
- **Base URL:** `https://connect.routee.net`
- **Official documentation:** [Get total amount of bounces](https://docs.routee.net/reference/get-total-amount-of-bounces)
