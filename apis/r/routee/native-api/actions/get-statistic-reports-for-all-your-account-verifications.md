# Get Statistic Reports for all your account verifications with Routee

Retrieves statistic reports for all your account verifications from Routee.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/2step/reports`
- **Base URL:** `https://connect.routee.net`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Statistic Reports for all your account verifications](https://docs.routee.net/reference/verification-reports)
