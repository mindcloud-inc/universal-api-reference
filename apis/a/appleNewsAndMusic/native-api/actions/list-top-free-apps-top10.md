# List Top Free Apps (Top 10) with Apple News and Music

Retrieves the top 10 free apps from App Store charts.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/us/apps/top-free/10/apps.json`
- **Base URL:** `https://itunes.apple.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Top Free Apps (Top 10)](https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/)
