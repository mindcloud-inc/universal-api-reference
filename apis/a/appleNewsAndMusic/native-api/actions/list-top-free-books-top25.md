# List Top Free Books (Top 25) with Apple News and Music

Retrieves the top 25 free books from Apple Books charts.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/us/books/top-free/25/books.json`
- **Base URL:** `https://itunes.apple.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Top Free Books (Top 25)](https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/)
