# List Apple Newsroom Articles with Apple News and Music

Retrieves Apple Newsroom articles from the official RSS feed.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://www.apple.com/newsroom/rss-feed.rss`
- **Base URL:** `https://itunes.apple.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Apple Newsroom Articles](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/rss-feed.rss)
