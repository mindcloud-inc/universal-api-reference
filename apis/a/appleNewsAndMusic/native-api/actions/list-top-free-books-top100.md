# List Top Free Books (Top 100) with Apple News and Music

Retrieves the top 100 free books from Apple Books charts.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/us/books/top-free/100/books.json`
- **Base URL:** `https://itunes.apple.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Top Free Books (Top 100)](https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/)
