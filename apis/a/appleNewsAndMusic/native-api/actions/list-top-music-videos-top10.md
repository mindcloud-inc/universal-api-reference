# List Top Music Videos (Top 10) with Apple News and Music

Retrieves the top 10 music videos from Apple Music charts.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/us/music/most-played/10/music-videos.json`
- **Base URL:** `https://itunes.apple.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Top Music Videos (Top 10)](https://rss.marketingtools.apple.com/)
