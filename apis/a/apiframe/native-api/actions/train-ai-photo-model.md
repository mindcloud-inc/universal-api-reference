# Train AI Photo Model with Apiframe

Creates an AI photo training task in Apiframe.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/ai-photo-train`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.apiframe.pro`
- **Official documentation:** [Train AI Photo Model](https://docs.apiframe.ai/ai-photos/train)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `training_images_id` | body | `string` | yes |
