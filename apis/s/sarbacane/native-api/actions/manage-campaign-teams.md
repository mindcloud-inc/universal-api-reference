# Manage Campaign Teams with Sarbacane

Updates campaign teams in your Sarbacane account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `PUT`
- **Path:** `/campaigns/{campaignId}/teams`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.sarbacane.com/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Manage Campaign Teams](https://developers.sarbacane.com/campaigns/#manage-campaigns-teams)
