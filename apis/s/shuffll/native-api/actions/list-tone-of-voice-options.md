# List Tone of Voice Options with Shuffll

Retrieves tone of voice options from Shuffll.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/auth/config/tone_of_voice`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.shuffll.com/api/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [List Tone of Voice Options](https://api-docs.shuffll.com/apis/configuration/gettoneofvoice)
