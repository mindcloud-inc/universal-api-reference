# Get Your Rights Online Feed with Slashdot

Retrieves the Your Rights Online feed from Slashdot.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `slashdotYourRightsOnline`
- **Base URL:** `https://rss.slashdot.org/Slashdot/`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Your Rights Online Feed](https://news.slashdot.org/faq/feeds.shtml)
