# Get CNB Monthly Cumulative Average Exchange Rates by Currency with Směnné kurzy ČNB

Retrieves monthly cumulative average exchange rates for a currency from Směnné kurzy ČNB.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/exrates/monthly-cumulative-averages-currency`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.cnb.cz/cnbapi`
- **Official documentation:** [Get CNB Monthly Cumulative Average Exchange Rates by Currency](https://api.cnb.cz/cnbapi/swagger-ui.html)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `currency` | query | `string` | yes |
