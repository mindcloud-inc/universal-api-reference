# List Suggested Edits On Posts with Stackoverflow

Retrieves suggested edits for specific posts from Stackoverflow.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/posts/[:ids]/suggested-edits`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.stackexchange.com/2.3`
- **Official documentation:** [List Suggested Edits On Posts](https://api.stackexchange.com/docs/posts-on-suggested-edits)
