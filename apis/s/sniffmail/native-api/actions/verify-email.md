# Verify Email with Sniffmail

Retrieves email verification results from Sniffmail.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/verify`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.sniffmail.io`
- **Official documentation:** [Verify Email](https://sniffmail.io/docs)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | body | `string` | yes | Enter the email address you want Sniffmail to verify. |
