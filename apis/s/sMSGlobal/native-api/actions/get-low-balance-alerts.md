# Get Low Balance Alerts with SMSGlobal

Retrieves low balance alert settings for the SMSGlobal account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v2/user/low-balance-alerts`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.smsglobal.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Low Balance Alerts](https://www.smsglobal.com/rest-api/)
