# Delete Contact By Email with SMASHSEND Email Marketing

Deletes a contact from SMASHSEND by email address.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `DELETE`
- **Path:** `/v1/contacts/by-email/:email`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.smashsend.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Delete Contact By Email](https://smashsend.com/docs/api/contacts)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | path | `string` | yes | The email address of the contact to delete. |
