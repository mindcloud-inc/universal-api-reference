# List WhatsApp Business Platform Highly Structured Templates with SeaX

Retrieves WhatsApp template options from SeaX by phone number.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/whatsapp_business_platform_templates/{phone_number}`
- **Base URL:** `https://seax.seasalt.ai/seax-api/api/v1/workspace/{workspaceId}`
- **Official documentation:** [List WhatsApp Business Platform Highly Structured Templates](https://api.seasalt.ai/seax/)
