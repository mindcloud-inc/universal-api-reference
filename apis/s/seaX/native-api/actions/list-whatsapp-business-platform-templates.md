# List WhatsApp Business Platform Templates with SeaX

Retrieves WhatsApp Business Platform templates from SeaX.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/whatsapp_business_platform/{service_provider_account_id}/templates`
- **Base URL:** `https://seax.seasalt.ai/seax-api/api/v1/workspace/{workspaceId}`
- **Official documentation:** [List WhatsApp Business Platform Templates](https://api.seasalt.ai/seax/)
