# Delete WhatsApp Business Platform Account with SeaX

Deletes a WhatsApp Business Platform account from SeaX.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `DELETE`
- **Path:** `/whatsapp_business_platform/{service_provider_account_id}`
- **Base URL:** `https://seax.seasalt.ai/seax-api/api/v1/workspace/{workspaceId}`
- **Official documentation:** [Delete WhatsApp Business Platform Account](https://api.seasalt.ai/seax/)
