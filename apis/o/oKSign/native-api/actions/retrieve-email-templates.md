# Retrieve Email Templates with OKSign

Retrieves email templates from OKSign.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/emailtemplates/retrieve`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.oksign.be/services/rest/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Retrieve Email Templates](https://www.oksign.be/public/api/#emailtemplates_retrieve)
