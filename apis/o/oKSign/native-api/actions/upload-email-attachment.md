# Upload Email Attachment with OKSign

Creates a new email attachment in OKSign.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/emailattachment/upload`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.oksign.be/services/rest/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Upload Email Attachment](https://www.oksign.be/public/api/#emailattachment_upload)
