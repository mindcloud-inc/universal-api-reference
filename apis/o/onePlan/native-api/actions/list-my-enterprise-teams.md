# List My Enterprise Teams with OnePlan

Retrieves your enterprise teams from OnePlan.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/enterpriseteams/list/my`
- **Base URL:** `https://my.oneplan.ai/api`
- **Official documentation:** [List My Enterprise Teams](https://my.oneplan.ai/ApiHelp/Api/GET-api-enterpriseteams-list-my)
