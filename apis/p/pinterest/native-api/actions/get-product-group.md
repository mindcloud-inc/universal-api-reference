# Get Product Group with Pinterest

Retrieves a product group from a Pinterest catalog.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `catalogs/product_groups/:productGroupId`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.pinterest.com/v5`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Product Group](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/api/v5/#operation/catalogs_product_groups/get)
