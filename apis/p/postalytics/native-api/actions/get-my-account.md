# Get My Account with Postalytics

Retrieves your authenticated Postalytics account details.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/api/v1/account/me`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.postalytics.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get My Account](https://docs.postalytics.com/references/postalytics-rest-api/get-my-account)
