# Get Account Product Counts with Parklio

Retrieves account product counts from Parklio.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/v2/accounts/product-counts`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.parklio.com`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Account Product Counts](https://api.parklio.com/api#/ACCOUNTS/AccountsController_getProductCountForProfile)
