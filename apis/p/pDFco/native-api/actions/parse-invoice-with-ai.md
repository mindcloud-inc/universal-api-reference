# Parse Invoice with AI with PDF.co

Parses an invoice with AI in PDF.co.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `POST`
- **Path:** `/ai-invoice-parser`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.pdf.co/v1`
- **Official documentation:** [Parse Invoice with AI](https://docs.pdf.co/api-tester/ai-invoice-parser)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `url` | body | `string` | yes | URL of invoice PDF/image to parse. |
