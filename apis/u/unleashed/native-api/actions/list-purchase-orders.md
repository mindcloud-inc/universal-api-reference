# List Purchase Orders with Unleashed

Retrieves purchase orders from your Unleashed account.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/PurchaseOrders/:pageNumber`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.unleashedsoftware.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Purchase Orders](https://apidocs.unleashedsoftware.com/Purchases)

## Capabilities

This operation supports [pagination](../README.md#pagination).
