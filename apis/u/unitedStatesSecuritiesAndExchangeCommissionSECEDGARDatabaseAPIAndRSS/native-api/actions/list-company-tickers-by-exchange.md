# List Company Tickers By Exchange with United States Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) EDGAR Database

Retrieves company ticker mappings by exchange from SEC EDGAR.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/files/company_tickers_exchange.json`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.sec.gov`
- **Official documentation:** [List Company Tickers By Exchange](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data)
