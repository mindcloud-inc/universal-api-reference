# Get M4.5+ Earthquakes Past Week with USGS Earthquake Hazards

Retrieves M4.5+ earthquakes from the past week.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/summary/4.5_week.geojson`
- **Base URL:** `https://earthquake.usgs.gov`
- **Official documentation:** [Get M4.5+ Earthquakes Past Week](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php)
