# Get Company Settings with Kladana

Retrieves company settings details from Kladana.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/context/companysettings`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.kladana.com/api/remap/1.2`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company Settings](https://dev.kladana.com/doc/api/remap/1.2/dictionaries/#entities-company-settings-get-company-settings)
