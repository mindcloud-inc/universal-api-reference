# Get Company Subscription with Kladana

Retrieves company subscription details from Kladana.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/accountSettings/subscription`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.kladana.com/api/remap/1.2`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Company Subscription](https://dev.kladana.com/doc/api/remap/1.2/dictionaries/#entities-company-subscription-get-company-subscription)
