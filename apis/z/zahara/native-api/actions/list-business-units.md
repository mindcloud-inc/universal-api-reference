# List Business Units with Zahara

Retrieves business units from a Zahara tenancy.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/api/{tenancyApiKey}/BusinessUnit/GetAll`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.myzahara.net`
- **Official documentation:** [List Business Units](https://ask.zaharasoftware.com/api-docs/get-business-units)
