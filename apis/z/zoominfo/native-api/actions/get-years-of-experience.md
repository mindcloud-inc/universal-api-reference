# Get Years Of Experience with Zoominfo

Retrieves years of experience values from ZoomInfo.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `lookup/years-of-experience`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.zoominfo.com/`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Years Of Experience](https://api-docs.zoominfo.com/#b9407739-7b4a-4283-8c4d-94d7ceb7cbbd)
