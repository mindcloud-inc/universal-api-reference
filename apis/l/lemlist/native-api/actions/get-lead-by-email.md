# Get Lead by Email with lemlist

Finds a lead in lemlist by email address.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/leads/:email`
- **Base URL:** `https://api.lemlist.com/api`
- **Official documentation:** [Get Lead by Email](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/leads/get-lead-by-email)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Location | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | path | `string` | yes | The email address of the lead to retrieve. |
