# List QR Codes with LinkTwin

Retrieves your QR codes from LinkTwin.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/qr`
- **Base URL:** `https://linktw.in/api`
- **Official documentation:** [List QR Codes](https://linktw.in/developers#list-qr-codes)

## Capabilities

This operation supports [pagination](../README.md#pagination).
