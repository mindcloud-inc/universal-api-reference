# List Climate and Environment Stories with Los Angeles Times

Retrieves Los Angeles Times climate and environment stories.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/environment/rss2.0.xml`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.latimes.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List Climate and Environment Stories](https://www.latimes.com/environment/rss2.0.xml)
