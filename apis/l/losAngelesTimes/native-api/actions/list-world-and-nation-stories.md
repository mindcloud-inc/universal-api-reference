# List World and Nation Stories with Los Angeles Times

Retrieves Los Angeles Times world and nation stories.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/world-nation/rss2.0.xml`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.latimes.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List World and Nation Stories](https://www.latimes.com/world-nation/rss2.0.xml)
