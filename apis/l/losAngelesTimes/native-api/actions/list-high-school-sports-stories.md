# List High School Sports Stories with Los Angeles Times

Retrieves Los Angeles Times high school sports stories.

## Endpoint

- **Method:** `GET`
- **Path:** `/sports/highschool/rss2.0.xml`
- **Base URL:** `https://www.latimes.com`
- **Official documentation:** [List High School Sports Stories](https://www.latimes.com/sports/highschool/rss2.0.xml)
